# GAME BIBLE — Beanie & Bred World 🐰🍞🌎🩷

## Why
A birthday gift for exactly one person: Beanie (Rachel). The game is a love letter
from Bred (Justin). Every decision must make Beanie feel more loved.

## North star
Gameplay: Super Mario Galaxy · Art: Animal Crossing · Story: The Little Prince
Emotion over complexity. Polish over features. Wonder over realism. Ship it.

## Story flow
> ⚠️ Stale — superseded by `STORYLINE.md` (post-reboot: world list/order,
> radii, mission designs, and the Cucumber Meadow reveal mechanic have all
> changed since this was written). Kept here for the original high-level
> pitch/tone only.
1. Title: dark pastel-blue space, gray Chicago planet rotating, Beanie sitting
   against a building. Press SPACE → third-person.
2. Worlds (each bigger than the last): Chicago (r7, gray, lonely) → Book Stacks
   (r9.5, Chippy napping) → Cucumber Meadow (r12, cucumbers school like fish) →
   The Deep Blue (r14, whales/fish/seahorses) → Pigeon Plaza (r16, too many
   pigeons; Percy) → The Garden (r22, reached only by riding Percy).
3. Percy sequence: talk (SPACE) → 4 dialogue lines → pigeons scatter → Percy
   grows → Beanie rides → iris-circle wipe → Garden.
4. Garden: light pastel-blue sky, birthday music starts HERE (cute music-box
   loop everywhere else), field FULL of flowers, white couch with Bred + Percy
   (puppy size) + Chippy. Meeting Bred → hearts → envelope → typed card → end.

## Core loop
Explore → aim view at a nearby moon/planet → SPACE to leap → discover the world
→ mission guides one interaction → progress dots (constellation) fill → repeat.

## Never add
Combat, timers, enemies, XP, inventory, minimap, monetization, procedural filler.
