# Mori Look Mechanics

Mori looks around by keeping both feet and the lower charcoal body planted while the large physical eyes lead, the ivory face and head turn or pitch next, and the upper torso follows subtly. The teal knit cap stays fitted to the head; its soft crown compresses slightly on downward looks and lifts slightly on upward looks. The coral thread loop remains attached at the same cap-side seam and lags the head motion by a small, continuous amount. Eye whites, dark irises, pupils, eyelids, and highlights rotate together as physical plush eye constructions rather than sliding pupils over a fixed surface.

Motion budget: each 22.5-degree step uses a similar small change in eye rotation, head yaw or pitch, and upper-body follow-through. Feet, lower-body center, baseline, body volume, cap identity, and thread attachment stay stable. No whole-sprite rotation, affine tilt, scale change, or independent prop jump.

Cardinal pose families:

- 000 up: eyes rotate upward, upper eyelids lift, chin rises, the ivory face pitches up, and a little more underside of the cap brim is visible. The cap crown follows upward; the coral loop hangs slightly behind the movement.
- 090 screen-right: eyes and nose move toward screen-right, the head yaws right, the screen-left cheek becomes more visible, and the screen-right cheek compresses slightly. The right side of the cap turns away; the attached coral loop remains physically connected and becomes a little more side-on.
- 180 down: eyes rotate downward, upper lids lower slightly, chin tucks, face pitches down, and more cap crown becomes visible. The upper body compresses gently while the planted base remains unchanged.
- 270 screen-left: eyes and nose move toward screen-left, the head yaws left, the screen-right cheek becomes more visible, and the screen-left cheek compresses slightly. The coral loop follows the cap seam continuously and may become more visible without switching sides.

Diagonals interpolate both axes evenly. Adjacent poses preserve the same anchored feet, body registration, eye construction, facial proportions, cap fit, and coral attachment. The 157.5 to 180 and 337.5 to 000 boundaries each advance by exactly one visual step.
